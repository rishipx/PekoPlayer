# Multi-view

Multi-view shows several local videos and YouTube links at once. Add videos with the toolbar, drag files onto the wall, or use **Folder** to add every video in a folder.

## Layouts

| Layout | What it does | Arranging and resizing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Canvas** | Free placement: tiles can overlap. | Drag a tile by its header, resize it from its edges and corners. |
| **Grid** | Packs tiles into rows, each at its video's aspect ratio, so nothing is cropped. | Drag a tile by its header to reorder. Drag a handle between tiles or rows to resize. |
| **Split** | Equal columns and rows that fill the wall. | Drag a tile by its header to reorder. Drag a handle between columns or rows to resize. |

## Reordering

- Press and drag a tile's header. The tile follows the pointer, the others make room, and a dashed outline shows the slot it will take. Release to drop it. **Esc** while dragging puts everything back.
- With a tile active, press **[** to move it one place earlier and **]** one place later. This is the keyboard alternative to dragging.
- The order is saved and restored the next time you open the wall.
- In Grid and Split, sizes belong to the slots, not the tiles: a tile moved into a larger slot becomes larger.

## Resizing

Thin handles sit in the gaps between tiles and light up when you hover them.

- Drag a handle to resize. Only the two panes next to it change, and each keeps at least 120 px, so nothing overlaps and nothing is cropped. Videos stay fully visible and are letterboxed when a pane no longer matches their shape.
- Where a column handle and a row handle cross, dragging moves both.
- With a handle focused (Tab to it), the arrow keys move it, and Shift makes the steps four times larger.
- Double-click a handle, or press **Home** or **Enter** on it, to reset: equal columns and rows in Split, automatic packing in Grid.
- Sizes are saved. They reset on their own when the number of tiles or columns changes.

## Fullscreen

The expand button on a tile shows that tile fullscreen without a frame or accent border. Its controls and the pointer hide after a short idle time and come back when the pointer moves.
